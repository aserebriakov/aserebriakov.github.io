Code to the Rescue — Support

For iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Code to the Rescue is a coding game for kids: build programs from simple blocks and watch a team of rescuers follow them, one step at a time, to help the animals of a wildlife reserve.

Contact

Questions, problems, or ideas? Send a message here (it opens a form on GitHub). Please mention your device and, if something went wrong, the mission you were playing.

How do I play?

Pick a mode, open a mission and open its program. Drag commands such as Forward, Turn and Action into the program, then press ▶. Your rescuers carry out one command per step. If the run goes wrong, step through it one command at a time to see where, fix the block, and run again. A short tutorial plays on the first mission, and a tip appears the first time something new shows up.

How do I see the tutorial again?

On the start screen, tap Replay tutorial. Your mission progress is kept.

Which missions are free?

Each of the three modes — Solo Rescue, Rescue Squad, and Bug Hunt — has 25 missions. The first 5 of every mode are free. A mode's pack unlocks its missions 6–25, and the bundle unlocks all three modes. Purchases are shared with your family through Family Sharing and work on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

I bought a pack on another device. How do I get it here?

Open Settings (the gear on the start screen) and choose Restore Purchases under For parents. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

Does my progress sync?

Yes. When iCloud is on, your progress and stars sync between your iPhone, iPad, and Mac through your own iCloud account. Progress made on two devices is combined, so nothing is lost.

Can I change the language?

Yes. In Settings, choose Language. The game supports 14 languages and switches the next time it opens.

Why do some buttons ask a maths question?

Purchases and links that leave the game sit behind a parental gate, so they are meant for a grown-up. Answer the question to continue.

Privacy

The game collects no personal data, shows no ads, and has no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
